* Research Interest

*Neuro-Symbolic Systems (Machine Learning + Classical AI) for search/planning/reasoning*,
with an emphasis on Bayesian generative modeling (e.g., variational inference).
This includes:

1. *Automatic identification of discrete structured representation for planning* (symbol grounding and knowledge acquisition).
2. *Learning a search policy / heuristics* for faster problem solving.
3. *Statistically well-motivated search algorithms* inspired by multi-armed bandits.

Recent work also focuses on LLM-based reasoning, inference-time scaling, and algorithmic guarantees while using LLMs.
